01 · Research background & problem

Does a mixed-use city remain accessible throughout the day?

Mixed in space is not mixed in time.

Research background

Mixed-use planning records which functions coexist, not when their amenities are open. In Singapore's compact Central Area, offices and schools close in the evening while only some food and retail stay open, so an area that looks mixed on the map can offer far less after dark.

Research aim

Relate land-use mix to 24-hour amenity availability in three catchments, as a basis for a model that can be applied more widely.

Planning relevance

Identify periods of limited amenity availability and provide evidence for comparing mixed-use areas.

No planning-oriented framework yet links land-use composition to 24-hour amenity availability, or uses that link to compare mixed-use scenarios.

03 · What we found

Open alike by day, apart after dark.

Mix ≠ night-time availability. After 22:00 only the least-mixed area falls clearly behind.

On weekdays, 80–88% of amenities with known hours are open between 09:00 and 18:00 in all three districts. Between 22:00 and 05:00, Bencoolen and Tanjong Pagar keep about 20% open, Promenade 9.5%.

Weighting by GFA lowers the night share from 18.4% to 15.6%: high-GFA parcels are less active at night.

“For evening and night-shift workers, students and city-centre residents, a service that is nearby but closed when needed is not a real opportunity.” Choose a person and a time to see what is still open within each 400 m catchment.
