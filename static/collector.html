    Study areas

    Discover POIs automatically

    Queries OpenStreetMap (Overpass API, free) for every amenity, shop, tourism, leisure, craft and healthcare object inside each selected area, removes non-functional objects (benches, parking, toilets, ATMs …) and classifies the rest into the six functional categories.

    Selected areas: 0

    Retrieve opening hours

    For each POI the collector tries, in order: the OSM opening_hours tag → Google Places (optional) → the business website and web search results. Text is turned into a weekly schedule by structured-data parsing, rules and (optional) an AI model. Every value is stored with its source URL and the exact evidence text.

    Google check queue

    For POIs the automatic sources could not fill. Each POI opens its Google search in a side tab; copy the opening-hours table from Google’s place panel (English or Chinese) and paste it here. The text is parsed automatically and saved as a researcher-verified record with the Google link as evidence.

    Tip: put this window and the Google tab side by side. Keyboard: Ctrl+Enter save & next · Ctrl+→ skip.

    Records & evidence

    Validate accuracy

    Three checks, reported separately so the accuracy of retrieved — and estimated — hours is measured, not assumed.

    ① Manual audit (stratified random sample)

    Draws unaudited records at random from each source/method. Check each against the place itself (official page, Google Maps listing, a phone call or a site visit) and mark it. Accuracy per method is reported with 95 % Wilson intervals. Incorrect records are removed from the final value; a correction becomes a researcher-verified record.

    ② Cross-source agreement

    For POIs with two independent sources, the share of the 168 weekly hours on which they agree. Keep “cross-check” on in Settings to collect a second source.

    ③ Estimation back-test

    Leave-one-out: hide each POI with known hours, estimate it from same-type peers, compare. This quantifies the bias that estimated hours would introduce into a model.

    Estimated records stay labelled estimated and are left out of Explore and exports’ availability profiles unless you tick them in.
